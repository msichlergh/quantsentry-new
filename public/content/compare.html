How the market compares

You were sold rules.
You bought a rule checker.

Most firms already believe risk is covered, because the CRM has a rules module. Rules enforce a challenge: drawdown, daily loss, targets, consistency. They are useful and they are not detection. A rules module checks one account against a number. It cannot see six accounts working as one, and that is where the money goes.

The uncomfortable part

Marketing built these firms.
Risk is what keeps them.

The sector grew on acquisition, because revenue came from failed challenges and the cheapest lever was more traffic. That worked until the good traders arrived organised. Between 2024 and 2025 roughly one in seven firms left the market, and the ones that stayed are the ones that could tell you what was actually happening in their book.

The test

Can you say, right now, how many accounts on your book are working together, and what they are holding in requested payouts? If the answer takes a week, that is the gap.

What it is not

Not a compliance project, not a regulator requirement, and not a reason to slow anything down. It is the difference between a payout you argue about and one you decline in writing.
