How Pricing Works

A clear pricing model.
A quote for your business.

Priced on active accounts

Monthly active trading accounts, not seats. Nobody pays for a login, and adding a risk reviewer costs nothing.

Everything on every plan

Trading Abuse Detection, every Evidence Kit and Argus AI. No locked features, no engine add ons and no module you discover you needed later.

Onboarding is one month

A one off fee equal to a month of subscription. It is a read-only key and a mapping session, not an implementation project.

Commit and save

Monthly is standard. Three, six and twelve month commitments carry a discount, agreed up front rather than negotiated at renewal.

No per seat pricing, no revenue share, and no percentage of your payouts. Outgrowing a tier is an account count, not a renegotiation.

What Is Included

Every plan,
the whole platform.

Every plan includes the full platform, so your team can review all supported risk patterns without feature limits.

What we need to quote you
  • Roughly how many accounts are trading in a month
  • Which platforms you run
  • Whether your team works the queue or we do
  • Whether you want a commitment or month to month

Four answers and you get a number, usually on the same call. We would rather quote you properly than publish a table you have to argue with.
