What the Layer Does

Three things,
on one set of data.

Rules check whether two things are equal. We measure how far apart they are, across roughly 130 variables per trade, so changing two of them does not break the link.

Resolve the identities

Device, session, payment and behavioural links resolve many accounts into the operators actually behind them, whatever they declared at signup.

Score the behaviour

Roughly 130 variables per trade, scored 0 to 1000 by distance rather than by rule, so changing two of them does not break the link.

Prove it in writing

Every finding arrives as an evidence kit, cited against the clause it breaches in your own terms, ready for the person who disputes it.
